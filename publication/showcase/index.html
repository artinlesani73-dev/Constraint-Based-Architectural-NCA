02 / Approach

A local rule, inside a larger system.

G10 / Learned baseline

Learned proposals with deterministic connected cube admission and paced budget control. A hybrid baseline in its own right.

R3 / Planned + learned

A thick route witness and coverage plan coordinate learned additions under guards and a budget.

Neutral / Procedural control

The same R3 planner and guards, with constant occupancy logits and zero hidden updates. Both learned gating and ranking are removed.

The goal is volumetric mass, with interiors and construction left for later. Passing a geometric test does not establish architectural quality or building safety.

03 / Evidence

Better acceptance.
An open question about learning.

Learning is not demonstrated necessary for these acceptance gates: the neutral control also passed every feasible request. R3 changes form and seed variation; greater variation alone is not evidence of better design.

Each study also includes two blocked requests per method. G10 produced rejected outputs; R3 and neutral refused. Refusals are not passes. Transfer covers four authored geometries and two seeds, with all 24 slots completed. This is a small sample, not a generalization guarantee.

04 / Research workflow

A studio that preserves the trail.

The private local studio supports generation, saved histories, versioned drafts and notes, metre-based OBJ exports, and recoverable partial evidence. Interrupted attempts remain in the record. Retrying creates a new identity; it does not resume neural state.

The frozen model and studies remain separate from presentation. This public-facing draft contains saved geometry and a technical report, with no private run history, checkpoint, data submission or live compute.
