# Spatial meaning before another training phase

2026-09-24. Trigger: the user observed that Studio output looks like a thin element
rather than a space. This is a correct diagnosis of the present representation and
objectives. This brief is a proposed research gate, not an accepted new metric or
implemented architectural generator. D026 material/form scope and nine families
remain in force. It precedes the learning pilot in PLANNER_REFINER_SPEC.md.

## What the displayed result is

Studio runs W1 procedural construction, not the trained NCA. The historical
checkpoint supplies configuration, but its learned weights do not generate the
Studio geometry. F5 remains an unpromoted research result:66/72 connected final
evaluations,0/72 within budget and0/72 joint. Those counts are local evaluation
cases, not independent buildings or evidence about unseen sites.

A sparse chain of occupied cells can satisfy connected-material access while
using little material. At0.8m per cell, a one-cell-wide element is0.8m wide in the
voxel representation. Its route need not describe a traversable surface. Existing
thickness/support proxies do not prove usable width, headroom or structural safety.
Empty space, walkable surface and solid material have different meanings; a scalar
occupied field does not identify them automatically. Zero proxy penalties therefore
do not prove an inhabitable architectural result. This is more than a rendering issue.

## Recommended bounded next design task

Start with a usable connecting platform or passage between buildings, before
claiming complete rooms/buildings. This is a recommendation requiring a concrete
brief, not a user-approved change to nine-family definitions. Draw explanatory
plan/section examples of the intended material, usable surface and surrounding
clear volume, including one thin connected failure and one spatially useful case.
Specify dimensions in metres and voxel resolution explicitly; distinguish desired
design dimensions from any jurisdiction-specific code compliance.

Audit whether derived surface/clearance masks from a solid field suffice, or whether
explicit material/surface/void channels are necessary. Compare these representations
before changing the NCA. Initial controls should be procedural spatial construction
and direct optimization, each evaluated on the same scenes and metric version.

Refine meanings within the existing families only: access could evaluate traversal
of usable surface with clearance; thickness could evaluate declared element width
or depth; support remains geometric until a mechanical method is actually supplied.
Coverage/sparsity must specify what material counts relative to which fixed domain.
Test their compatibility with platform dimensions before carrying over the3–12%
budget. No numerical target or old metric is changed by this proposal. Version any
future definition and keep all legacy scores and source artifacts for comparison.

Create independent geometric examples, negatives and feasibility checks before
learning. Choose an NCA refinement/recovery task only after controls produce the
intended kind of space and there is a measurable benefit for learned refinement.
Increasing the voxel count can improve discretization after this contract is clear;
it cannot supply spatial meaning absent from the task. Larger/diverse sites should
follow correctness and profiling, with held-out site families for generalization.

Next concrete deliverable: a short illustrated spatial contract with candidate
representation, dimensions, within-family metric mapping, compatibility audit and
procedural examples. Then revise/freeze the task and only subsequently consider
the gated Colab pilot. No additional paid compute or Drive action is needed now.
