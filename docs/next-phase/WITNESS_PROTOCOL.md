# W1_v1 constructive witness protocol

Post-A1 follow-up, frozen before W1 outcomes. A1 has no simple tested zero-loss
witness in six of17 feasible scenes despite necessary bound compatibility. Test
budgeted_witness_v1 on all18, not only failures. Separate run; do not add W1 to A1.

Fixed radius6/envelope3%-12%, facade_endpoint_v1, guide, all nine formulas and
64-hop support/access unchanged. Begin at full binary guide. Required mass is
ceil(max(3% envelope capacity,guide count,charged guide contact/15%)). Refuse when
that integer exceeds12% envelope capacity or the initial guide already has a
radius2 eroded core. Add only uncharged, legal, face-adjacent cells inside the
unchanged envelope, in lexicographic z,y,x order, rejecting new eroded cores.
Stop at target mass or explicit frontier exhaustion; no retries with wider bounds.
Record ordered added cells, before/after fields, all nine raw terms and independent
binary metrics. The sealed reference must remain incompatible. No optimizer.

This is deliberately a procedural objective-satisfaction baseline, not a claim of
architectural quality. Adding mass can dilute a contact ratio, exposing that
objective's remaining weakness. Directional tie-breaking may bias shape and will
not be presented as learned design. Even a zero-loss witness must be compared
visually and architecturally; no structural-safety/walkability inference.

Acceptance: every claimed witness independently replays, keeps original guide,
legal region/budget/other terms, has all nine terms<=1e-7, and is connected under
independent binary metrics. Anything else stays a measured failure. Report all18.
