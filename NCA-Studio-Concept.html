The proposed generation flow keeps every result reproducible and every failure visible.

  1. Validate the scene. Check entrance identities, units and the agreed design region.
  2. Queue a generation job. Record the scene revision, checkpoint and effective seed.
  3. Grow and refine. Show progress; cancellation stops the worker.
  4. Evaluate independently. Inspect occupied material and usable empty space.
  5. Compare and save. Keep alternatives, diagnostics and export settings together.
