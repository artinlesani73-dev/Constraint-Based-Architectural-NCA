WHAT ARE WE MEASURING?

Extent, material and void are different.

Blue marks empty cells with proposed material somewhere on both sides along at least two coordinate axes. This includes some open voids. It is a diagnostic mask, not generated material or a universal definition of space.

A solid block can have depth without an internal void. A hollow form can contain spatial depth with thin walls. Scattered cells can imitate the same outer extents.

Closed cavities are counted separately. Existing buildings can close an otherwise open form; the two closure counts expose that difference. Measures depend on voxel resolution, axis orientation and the sampled boundary.
