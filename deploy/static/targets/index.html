THE PILOT CONTRACT

Depth must be present
in the geometry.

At least 90% of the mass must belong to complete cubes at least 2.4 m across. Those substantial regions must connect the declared interfaces. A narrow neck cannot substitute for that connection.

The volume budget is 8–40% of a fixed scene-derived region. Each of its three X-direction thirds must contain at least 8% substantial volume.

These are declared experimental settings, not architectural standards. An axis-aligned cube check can miss other useful forms. Passing all nine checks does not measure architectural quality.
