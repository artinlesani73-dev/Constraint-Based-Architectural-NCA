What this preview proves — and what it does not

Generate new paired outputs on the listed sites, or inspect the saved evaluation cases. Local runs are exploratory and do not extend the independent evaluation score. R3 reuses G10's trained weights and adds a geometry planner plus cumulative growth allowance. No new model was trained for R3. The planner forces a thick connection and minimum coverage; later growth still follows neural scores subject to global guards.

All nine constraint families passed for R3 on 69 previous cases and 12 new requests across four related sites, at both horizons. Raw G10 passed 51/69 and 2/12. The 12 requests are correlated, and are now exposed evaluation evidence. One firing seed, 32³ cells, 0.8 m per voxel. Zero late R3 growth reflects a hard volume cap, not independently learned stability.

Voxels represent overall building mass. This does not certify rooms, usable interiors, construction or mechanical safety. Views show exact saved occupancy without smoothing, filling or repair. Site outlines are an overlay and may appear through the generated mass.
